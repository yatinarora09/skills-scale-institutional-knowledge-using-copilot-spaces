# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads lead quality assurance efforts and ensure that features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They work closely with developers and product managers to define and execute comprehensive testing strategies.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Conduct manual and automated testing across features
- Identify and log defects with clear reproduction steps
- Collaborate with Developers to resolve quality issues
- Validate fixes and sign off on feature quality
- Contribute to Definition of Done and test automation strategy
- Mentor team members on quality best practices

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce production incidents
- Maintain high quality and customer satisfaction
- Enable faster releases through reliable test coverage
- Build a culture of quality ownership across the team

### Interactions
- Works with Developers on test design and acceptance criteria clarity
- Reports status and quality metrics to Product Manager and Project Manager
- Partners with Developers and Technical Architect on test infrastructure and automation
- Participates in sprint planning to understand testing requirements early

### Typical Communication
- QA status updates in daily standups
- Defect reports and quality dashboards
- Test plan reviews with developers and product managers
- Release readiness assessments

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects ensure that technical decisions align with long-term strategy, scalability, and maintainability. They provide guidance on system design, technology selections, and technical risk management.

### Responsibilities
- Review and advise on architectural decisions for major features
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Guide design discussions and technology selections
- Ensure consistency with established patterns and standards
- Mentor Developers on best practices and design principles
- Support capacity planning and performance optimization
- Maintain technical documentation and architectural decisions

### Goals
- Build a scalable, maintainable platform
- Reduce technical debt and future rework
- Enable team velocity through clear architectural guidance
- Balance innovation with stability

### Interactions
- Partners with Developers on design and code reviews
- Advises Project Manager on technical risks, dependencies, and feasibility estimates
- Collaborates with QA Lead on testability, performance requirements, and non-functional testing
- Works with Product Manager to understand technical implications of roadmap decisions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review comments and mentoring sessions
- Technical risk updates in project syncs
- Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approvals, and high-level direction for projects. They ensure alignment with organizational strategy and business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charters and funding
- Escalate critical blockers and risks that require executive intervention
- Provide business context and success metrics for projects
- Approve major scope changes or trade-offs
- Communicate project status and outcomes to executive leadership
- Remove organizational barriers to project success

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Minimize scope creep and maintain focus on strategic priorities
- Enable fast decision-making on trade-offs and priority conflicts
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and support

### Interactions
- Receives regular status updates and milestone reports from Project Manager
- Participates in project kick-off and major milestone reviews
- Acts as escalation point for Project Manager when team-level decisions are insufficient
- Provides input on business priorities and success metrics to Product Manager

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Executive summary reports
- Approval and sign-off on major milestones
- Escalation discussions as needed

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and help the team continuously improve its process. They enable team velocity and foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, retrospectives, and other ceremonies
- Identify and help remove team blockers and impediments
- Coach the team on agile principles, practices, and mindset
- Maintain sprint health metrics and dashboards
- Adapt ceremonies and practices based on team feedback and needs
- Support the team in continuous improvement and process optimization
- Model psychological safety and encourage open communication

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and throughput
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety
- Remove distractions so the team can focus on delivery
- Build team resilience and adaptability

### Interactions
- Works closely with Project Manager on scheduling, execution rhythm, and sprint planning
- Coaches Developers, Product Manager, Project Manager, and other roles on agile practices
- Facilitates communication and collaboration between team and stakeholders
- Partners with Technical Architect to help the team adopt technical excellence practices

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of all agile ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching and mentoring
- Retrospective action item tracking and follow-up
- Team health and velocity metrics
